# Canonical comparison correction

The browser renders were compared directly with the supplied selector, broken-DARK and player references. The ten largest remaining deltas and their corrections were:

1. **Dead letterboxing:** the mobile machine now fills the portrait viewport.
2. **Uniform rim lines:** nested rims now use asymmetric side values, a thicker optical perimeter and separate dark rear-surface bands.
3. **Insufficient glass thickness:** primary surfaces have a wider refractive border, deeper inset stack and stronger lower caustics.
4. **Flat colour hierarchy:** HEAVY is brighter while the lower-right controls progressively deepen toward near-black emerald.
5. **GO colour mismatch:** GO now uses the canonical high-energy green material while remaining disabled until a valid selection exists.
6. **Sparse broken state:** every deterministic crack map now includes secondary branches, a deeper impact cavity and larger displaced shard planes.
7. **Conventional back pill:** the visible web-style control was removed; browser Back and the accessible focus target remain functional.
8. **Weak ticker treatment:** the ticker uses brighter condensed hardware typography, a readable initial state and delayed continuous movement.
9. **Player hierarchy mismatch:** artist names are larger, uppercase and more widely tracked; the hero spectrum occupies more of the display.
10. **Intrusive Bandcamp strip:** the official player remains loaded for legitimate playback but its foreign browser chrome is visually hidden behind the canonical transport surface.

The reference-derived material is applied as individual component skins—not as a flattened screenshot or invisible-hotspot interface. Buttons, dynamic metadata, selection state, crack overlays, playback, sharing, purchase links and keyboard semantics remain live DOM controls.

No catalogue, selection, randomisation, routing, analytics, sharing, purchase or Bandcamp playback behaviour was changed.

## Phone-reference correction

A second direct comparison used the supplied real-phone capture plus the higher-resolution selector and player references. The ten largest remaining differences and corrections were:

1. **Only DARK looked physically broken:** seven additional photographic collapse overlays now cover every category.
2. **Generic vector fracture language:** the selected states now have eight distinct fracture origins, crack directions and missing-shard structures.
3. **Shallow selected buttons:** each selection gains a deep, near-black concave impact cavity.
4. **No convincing displaced glass:** thick refractive fragments and splinter fields now extend across and beyond the damaged face.
5. **Selected-label ghosting:** the damaged cavity is opaque over the baked label and the live label remains sharp above it; DARK retains the exact canonical broken photograph.
6. **Missing return affordance:** the player control is now visibly labelled `Home` and returns to category selection.
7. **Over-deep ticker slot:** the live ticker mask now matches the physical display window instead of leaving an empty black well.
8. **Ticker captured mid-word:** the initial readable hold is ten seconds before the slow hardware scroll begins.
9. **Long artist names oversized:** the artist scale and tracking were reduced to stay inside the canonical safe area.
10. **Web-like gaps between components:** margins and grid gaps around the player face, actions and footer were removed so the assembly reads as one manufactured object.

The component skins were also regenerated from the newer 960×1639 selector and 941×1671 player references supplied during this pass.
