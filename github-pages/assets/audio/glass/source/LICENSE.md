# Glass interaction source licences

All four recordings in this directory are published under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They may be copied, modified, distributed, and performed, including commercially, without attribution.

Source and creator details are recorded in `metadata.json`. Attribution is retained voluntarily for provenance and auditability.

The `.ogg` and `.mp3` files are Freesound's high-quality preview transcodes of the identified CC0 recordings. Cosmic Aquaria schedules only short regions and applies runtime filtering, timing, gain, pitch, and stereo treatment. No generated noise or oscillator is part of the glass-break effect.
